Fred Hutch Leader Competencies

Leader Competencies

Apply to all people managers at Fred Hutch — click any competency to expand.

  • Provides a compelling vision and direction.
  • Creates a climate where each team member is motivated to do their best to achieve goals.
  • Manages conflict effectively.
  • Forms teams with appropriate and diverse mix of styles, perspectives, and experience.
  • Fosters open dialogue and collaboration within and among teams.
  • Defines success in terms of the whole team, shares wins and rewards team efforts.

Development ideas

  • Places a high priority on developing others.
  • Nurtures the skills and abilities of all people according to their specific needs.
  • Fosters a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
  • Develops others through coaching, feedback, exposure, and stretch assignments.
  • Aligns employee career development goals with organizational objectives.
  • Encourages people to accept developmental moves.

Development ideas

  • Understands the connections and dynamics between groups.
  • Demonstrates sensitivity to how organizations and people function.
  • Understands other group's priorities and balances needs of stakeholders.
  • Identifies opportunities and obstacles and develops plans to work through them.
  • Steers through the organizational maze to get things done.
  • Uses knowledge of organizational culture to achieve objectives.
  • Understands the broader systems in which the organization operates.
  • Knows how the non-profit life sciences and healthcare industries work.

Development ideas

  • Plans and prioritizes work to align with broader organizational goals.
  • Provides clear expectations, direction, and accountabilities.
  • Steps up to hold difficult conversations.
  • Delegates and distributes assignments and decisions appropriately.
  • Provides appropriate guidance and direction based on people's capabilities.
  • Facilitates dialogue with and between team members to track progress.
  • Removes obstacles to help the team achieve goals.

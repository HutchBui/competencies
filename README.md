# Competencies

Self-assessment tools for Fred Hutch Core and Leader Competencies. Employees and people managers use these to reflect on how often they demonstrate key behaviors, view results by competency, and print a personal report.

## Files

- **core_exit_closetab_WITH_EXCEL_SUBMIT_Test.html** — Core Competencies self-assessment (all employees). Covers 8 competencies across 42 statements: Exhibits Accountability, Demonstrates Change Agility, Collaborates, Communicates Effectively, Interpersonal Savvy, Results-Oriented, Demonstrates Self-Awareness, and Instills Trust.
- **leader_exit_closetab_WITH_EXCEL_SUBMIT_Test.html** — Leader Competencies self-assessment (people managers). Covers 4 competencies across 27 statements: Builds Effective Teams, Develops Talent, Organizational Savvy, and Plans and Directs Work.

## How it works

Each tool presents an intro screen with instructions and an important disclaimer, then walks through each competency as a set of 1–5 rating statements ("Almost Never" to "Almost Always"). After completing all statements, results display as bar charts by competency and can be printed for personal reference. Anonymized competency averages are submitted to a Power Automate workflow for aggregate reporting; no personal information is tracked.

## Disclaimer

Both tools include a notice that they are reflection tools, not validated psychometric instruments, and are intended to inform — not determine — development priorities. They are not used for hiring, promotion, compensation, or other formal employment decisions, and results are not shared with HR or a manager unless the employee chooses to share them.
